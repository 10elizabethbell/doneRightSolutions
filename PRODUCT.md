# Product: Done Right Solutions LLC (Outdoor Services) website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`, images in `img/`), hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Live:** https://10elizabethbell.github.io/doneRightSolutions/

## Users
- People who need concrete poured: basement floors, garage slabs, patios. They reach the page from a Facebook post or link, on a phone, usually inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner, Kasey, who gets a free website as a first impression.

## Purpose
Turn "Need concrete work done?" into a call or text to Kasey for a free estimate.

## Positioning
A concrete contractor whose name is the promise: the job done right. The proof is the job itself, shown stage by stage (packed base, barrier and steel, pour, finished slab).

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: call or text (937) 407-9540. Texts accepted (stated in their post).
- No email, no booking system, no backend. The quote form composes a pre-written text message.
- Photo-upload intake (suggested by Muse) can't be done with `sms:` links; the form asks visitors to add a photo in the text instead.
- **No location on the page.** The 937 area code is Ohio but the ad ran in an Essex County NJ group. The Facebook page title says "Done Right Solutions LLC | Urbana OH" (fetched 2026-10-19), but the brief says to keep the site location-free until the owner confirms.

## Brand commitments
- Name: "Done Right Solutions LLC", with "Outdoor Services" on the logo's banner (logo confirms the suffix is their own branding).
- Colours from the logo: brick red check (#a92403), slate circle (#333a43), white ground. (inferred: the slate is mapped onto the calm style's navy roles)
- Tone: upbeat tradesman, plain. "We're ready to get your project done right!"

## Evidence on hand
- Services, verbatim: "From basement floors to garage slabs to patios and more"
- Offer, verbatim: "Call or text Kasey at (937) 407-9540 today to get your FREE estimate and get on the schedule!"
- Logo: Facebook profile picture.
- Photos: frames from one job video posted with their sign-off (an insulated-form floor slab: base, vapor barrier and wire mesh, pour, power trowel, finished slab with saw cuts).
- No reviews, prices, hours, years in business, or email.

## Principles
- Only facts from the brief and their own posts; nothing invented.
- Phone first.
- Owner's voice ("we", Kasey named as the contact).
